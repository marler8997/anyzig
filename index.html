anyzig

Run any version of zig. The version comes from your project's build.zig.zon, and anyzig downloads it the first time it's needed.

terminal
~/my-app$ zig version
0.17.0
~/old-lib$ zig version
0.15.2
Also want zls?anyzls runs the zls compatible with your project's zig version.

It's a separate download so installing anyzig never replaces your own zls. Put it in your PATH as zls or point your editor at it. See the README.
